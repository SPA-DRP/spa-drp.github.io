Motivated by various topics in statistics for genetics, in this DRP we investigate statistical inference for two quantities: relative risk, and the odds ratio. Each of these quantities conveys the effect that a treatment has on a subject, relative to a baseline of not having received the treatment. Techniques explored include bootstrapping, and asymptotic methods such as the delta method.
